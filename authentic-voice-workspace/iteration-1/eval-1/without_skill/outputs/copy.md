## Hero

I write Rust and Elixir. I've been doing it since 2022, and both languages still get the better of me on a regular basis.

I'm a hobbyist, not a professional. I picked the two languages most likely to fight back, and I keep coming back anyway.

## About

I took up coding seriously in 2022 and, for reasons I can't fully justify, chose Rust and Elixir — the two languages most likely to make me feel stupid. Three years in, I'm still not good at either. The borrow checker and I have an ongoing, mostly one-sided relationship. I'd like to tell you the struggle makes it more rewarding, but really it just makes it more interesting.

Somewhere between the compiler errors, I run a Discord server. It's loud and it's about nothing — the conversations there are not productive, and I include myself in that. I also keep a blog that I update every few months, which is more of a public record of what I tried and where it went sideways than anything resembling a tutorial.

You're welcome in both places. There's not much here, but what there is is genuinely mine.
