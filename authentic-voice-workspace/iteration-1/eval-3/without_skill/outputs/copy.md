I learned Rust because I wanted to feel like a real systems programmer, and the
best excuse I could find was a timer that tells you when your pasta is done. It
works by accident more often than not — the elapsed time is slightly wrong, the
alarm is a little late, and yet somehow the noodles still come out al dente
more times than they probably should. I don't fully understand why it works,
and I suspect the borrow checker doesn't either, but between the two of us
we've got dinner covered. Mostly.
