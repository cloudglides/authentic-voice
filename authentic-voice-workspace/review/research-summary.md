Discord-Specific Findings (from arXiv 2401.04120)
==================================================
Key Discord AI Detection Markers (from the arXiv 2401.04120 paper):

1. **Prompt structure**: AI Discord messages generated via one-shot prompt:
   "You are someone with a ${hobby} hobby. Please describe your hobby ${hobby} in 3 sentences."
   - 3-sentence structure is a strong AI indicator for Discord descriptions
   - Humans write variable-length server descriptions

2. **Detection paradox**: 
   - Generation Z individuals cannot discern AI from human (p = 0.011)
   - BUT those with LOWER Discord familiarity are BETTER at spotting human text (p << 0.0001)
   - Less Discord experience = better AI detection

3. **Human text characteristics** (vs AI):
   - More prose and ordinary grammatical structures
   - Consistent use of prose, coherence, clarity, effective language
   - Distinct terminologies and contexts per hobby
   - Nuanced recognition of hobby-specific subtleties

4. **AI text characteristics**:
   - Generated via gpt-3.5-turbo one-shot prompt
   - 25 different hobbies evaluated (comprehensive coverage)
   - Posted via webhook on isolated Discord server
   - Same username and default profile image used for consistency

5. **Why less familiar users detect better**:
   - Users with Discord experience may accept AI-generated style as "normal"
   - Novices notice the artificial patterns more readily
   - Familiarity can blind users to AI tells

Key AI Text Markers (from arxiv-2510.05136 AIGT Survey)
=======================================================
Survey of 8 domains, 11 LLMs, 6 prompting strategies:

1. **Lexical diversity**: Human texts show greater variability than machine-generated
   - AI texts have lower TTR (Type-Token Ratio)
   - AI texts have smaller vocabulary size
   - AI texts have more repetition

2. **Style embeddings**: 
   - Capture less variability in poetry than other domains
   - Newer models output text that is similarly variable (homogenization)
   - Human-written texts display greater variation in features

3. **Syntactic structures**: 
   - Human texts tend to exhibit simpler syntactic structures
   - More diverse semantic content in human texts
   - Both human and AI texts show domain variability

4. **Burstiness** (sentence length variation):
   - Human writing contains mix of short, medium, and longer sentences
   - AI writing uses more simplistic, uniform sentence structures
   - "Burstiness" = key differentiator

5. **Vocabulary**:
   - AI texts have lower lexical diversity
   - Human texts have 4x more unique words than AI texts
   - AI texts repeat same words more often

6. ** discourse markers**:
   - Less frequent in AI text
   - When present, tend to be more repetitive
   - AI text has fewer modal expressions and epistemic markers

7. **Confidence/caution**:
   - AI text tends to be more confident/less cautious
   - Fewer hedge words (almost, in general, probable)
   - More booster words (certainly, undoubtedly, essential)

8. **Error rate**:
   - AI text has fewer grammatical errors
   - AI text is "more correct" than human writing
   - "No visible errors" can be an AI tell

9. **Vocabulary specificity**:
   - AI-favored words: intricate, valuable, exceptional, pivotal (adjectives)
   - AI-favored adverbs: primarily, thoroughly, subsequently, particularly
   - Human texts more nuanced and varied in emotional tone

10. **Nominalization**:
    - AI texts have higher degree of nominalization
    - More nouns derived from other word classes
    - AI texts contain more nouns, determiners, and adpositions

11. **Adjectives/adverbs**:
    - AI texts less likely to contain adjectives and adverbs
    - AI texts more formal and impersonal style
    - Human texts more Involved, more personal style

12. **Proper nouns and first-person pronouns**:
    - AI texts contain fewer proper nouns
    - AI texts contain fewer first-person pronouns (mitrović et al. 2023)
    - Male personal pronouns more prominent in AI text (muñoz-ortiz et al. 2024)

13. **Sentence structures**:
    - AI text: less comparative and superlative structures
    - AI text: more phrasal coordination
    - AI text: more present participial clauses
    - AI text: more sentences with that-clauses as subjects

14. **Readability**:
    - AI text produces lower readability scores
    - Human text requires higher Flesch-Kincaid grade level
    - AI text is "easier to read but lacks lexical diversity"

15. **Structural patterns**:
    - AI text: more coordination (conjunctions)
    - AI text: more present participial clauses
    - AI text: more sentences with that-clauses as subjects
    - AI text: fewer subordinate clause structures

Discord Server Description Humanization Rules (derived from research)
================================================================

Based on analysis of AI-generated Discord text detection:

1. **Avoid fixed sentence counts** 
   - Do NOT write exactly 3 sentences about a Discord server
   - Humans write variable-length descriptions: very short, very long, bullet points, mixed formats
   - AI tends toward prompt-driven structure (3 sentences, 5 sentences, etc.)

2. **Include discourse markers**
   - Casual markers: "idk," "ngl," "lol," "fr," "tbh," "actually," "so yeah"
   - AI text has fewer discourse markers or they're more formally connected
   - Research: Discord markers less frequent in AI text (Herbold et al. 2023; Simon et al. 2023)

3. **Add specific anchors, not broad statements**
   - Instead of "nothing deep": "sometimes people argue about linux distros at 3am," "usually just memes and anime discussion"
   - Instead of "random chat": specific types of conversation, inside jokes, specific topics
   - AI tends toward broad generalizations; humans include specific details

4. **Mix casual and specific vocabulary**
   - Combine casual Discord vocabulary ("dead ass," "no cap," "based") with specific references
   - AI tends toward more formal word choices even when trying to be casual
   - Research: AI-favored vocabulary identified (Bao et al. 2025): 100 adjectives, 100 adverbs

5. **Embrace "imperfect" grammar** (strategically)
   - Include minor grammatical imperfections, sentence fragments, run-ons
   - AI tends toward "perfect" grammar; humans have natural inconsistencies
   - But don't overdo it — strategic imperfection, not random errors

6. **Variable burstiness**
   - Mix sentence lengths unpredictably: some 2-word fragments, some 30+ word sentences
   - AI tends toward uniform sentence intervals
   - "Burstiness" = mix of short, medium, and longer sentences

7. **Include personal/inside references**
   - Specific memories, inside jokes, specific member counts, specific activity times
   - AI cannot reference personal experiences it hasn't had
   - Research: AI text lacks personal experiences and reflection

8. **Avoid the "hobby description" pattern**
   - Don't follow: "You are someone with a [hobby] hobby. Please describe your hobby [hobby] in 3 sentences."
   - This exact prompt structure generates detectable AI text on Discord
   - Humans describe servers organically, without prompt-driven structure

Key Markers to Avoid (AI Discord Text Tells)
============================================

| Marker | AI Tendency | Human Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Exactly 3 sentences | ⬆️ Prompt-driven | ⬇️ Variable length |
| "nothing deep" / "random chat" | ⬆️ Broad generalizations | ⬇️ Specific anchors |
| No discourse markers (idk, lol, fr) | ⬆️ Formally connected | ⬇️ Casual markers present |
| Uniform sentence length | ⬆️ Burstiness absent | ⬇️ Variable burstiness |
| "Perfect" grammar | ⬆️ Few errors | ⬇️ Strategic imperfections |
| Broad statements without specifics | ⬆️ Generalizations | ⬇️ Specific details |
| Fixed vocabulary patterns | ⬆️ Repeated words | ⬇️ Diverse word choices |
| No personal references | ⬆️ No experience | ⬇️ Personal memories |
